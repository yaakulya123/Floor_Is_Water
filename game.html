Floor Is Water

City Defense

🌊 Mission

Save the city from rising floodwaters! You have 90 seconds to protect as much land as possible.

⌨️ Controls

  • • Hover over any tile
  • • Press W to place wood barriers
  • • Press S to place stone barriers

🎯 Strategy

  • • Wood: Decays in ~4 seconds when water touches it
  • • Stone: Permanent barrier, blocks water indefinitely
  • • Water rises every few seconds
  • • Build barriers to protect critical areas

🏆 Victory

  • • Save >70% of land → Island Saved
  • • Save <70% of land → Island Submerged
