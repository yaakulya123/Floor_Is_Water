The Story

A catastrophic storm surge is threatening your coastal city. As the newly appointed Mayor, you must act fast. Your advisors are panicking, your citizens are counting on you, and the water level won't stop rising.

You have limited resources: wooden barriers that decay quickly and precious stone that holds forever. Every decision matters. Every second counts. The fate of thousands rests on your shoulders.

Can you outsmart the flood and save your city before time runs out?

Game Features

Time-Based Challenge

90 seconds of intense decision-making. Every moment counts as the water rises and your city's fate hangs in the balance.

Strategic Building

Choose between temporary wood barriers or permanent stone defenses. Limited resources force you to think strategically.

Dynamic Water System

Watch as water rises in real-time, breaking through weak barriers and testing your defensive strategies.

Immersive Storytelling

Your advisors provide real-time updates and urgent warnings as the situation develops, creating tension and urgency.

Real-Time Statistics

Track your progress with live percentage indicators showing how much land you've saved. Stay above 65% to win!

Skill-Based Gameplay

Easy to learn, challenging to master. Quick reflexes and smart planning are both essential for victory.

How to Play

W

Place Wood
Quick temporary barrier that decays in ~4 seconds when water touches it

S

Place Stone
Permanent barrier that blocks water indefinitely

Hover & Click
Move your mouse over any tile and press W or S to build

Victory Conditions

ISLAND SAVED

Save more than 65% of the land

Your strategic planning and quick reflexes paid off. The citizens celebrate as the majority of the city remains above water. You are a hero!

ISLAND SUBMERGED

Save less than 65% of the land

Despite your efforts, the flood consumed too much territory. The city sinks into the depths, and the people must evacuate. Perhaps next time...
