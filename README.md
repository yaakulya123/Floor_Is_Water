# Floor Is Water - City Defense

A tower defense-style game where you must save a pixelated city from flooding!

## How to Play

🌊 **Objective**: Keep as many city tiles dry as possible!

- **Wood barriers** (brown): Temporary protection, resist water for 4 seconds
- **Stone barriers** (black): Permanent protection, blocks water indefinitely
- **Water** (blue): Spreads every 2 seconds to adjacent tiles
- **Resources**: Wood and stone generate automatically over time

## Controls

- **Click** on land tiles to place barriers
- Use wood first (cheaper, temporary) or stone (permanent, limited)
- Strategic placement is key!

## Tech Stack

- **p5.js** for graphics and game engine
- **Custom tilemap system** with 8x8 pixel tiles
- **Real-time flood simulation** with barrier mechanics
